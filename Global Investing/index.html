About US NASDAQ Intelligence

GammaPace NASDAQ Intelligence is a next-generation investment analytics platform engineered to decode the world's most dynamic technology-driven marketplace. Our mission is to democratize institutional-grade financial intelligence, giving every investor—from first-time traders to seasoned portfolio managers—unparalleled visibility into the 3,700+ companies that power the NASDAQ Composite Index.

The platform aggregates and processes millions of data points daily, spanning real-time market feeds, SEC EDGAR filings, earnings transcripts, analyst consensus, insider trading activity, and alternative data sources such as news sentiment and social-media signals. Our proprietary AI engine transforms this raw data into actionable insights: buy/sell signals, price-target projections, volatility scoring, and sector-rotation alerts.

Four Pillars of Intelligence: Market & Trading Data delivers live quotes, depth-of-book analytics, and volume-profile studies. Company & Fundamental Data offers deep-dive financial statement analysis, valuation modeling, and peer benchmarking. Funds, Indices & Crypto tracks ETF flows, index rebalancing, and digital-asset correlations. Events, Ownership & News monitors earnings calendars, insider Form 4 filings, 13F institutional disclosures, and breaking news sentiment.

Whether you pursue growth, value, momentum, or income strategies, GammaPace adapts its analytics to your framework. Our risk-assessment layer quantifies drawdown potential, beta exposure, and liquidity constraints so you can allocate capital with confidence. All data is refreshed continuously, and alerts are pushed in real time to ensure you never miss a market-moving event.
